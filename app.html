Mafia Hearts KDrama App

Is website ko aap apne phone par bilkul ek app ki tarah install kar sakte hain. Home screen par gold "MH" icon aayega, ek tap mein website khulegi — na Play Store ki zaroorat, na alag download.

Android (Chrome) par install karein

1. Is page ko Chrome mein kholein.
2. Upar "Install App" wala button dabayen — ya Chrome ke menu (⋮) mein "Install app" / "Add to Home screen" chunein.
3. "Install" par tap karein. Ho gaya — icon aap ki home screen par hai.

iPhone (Safari) par install karein

1. Is page ko Safari mein kholein.
2. Share button dabakar "Add to Home Screen" chunein.
3. "Add" par tap karein.

App ke faide

• Ek tap mein naye episodes aur Shorts tak rasai
• Halki aur tez — phone ki memory na ke barabar leti hai
• Hamesha taaza — naye episodes khud bakhud nazar aate hain

Kadwa Sach App

Is website ko aap apne phone par bilkul ek app ki tarah install kar sakte hain. Home screen par gold "KS" icon aayega, ek tap mein website khulegi — na Play Store ki zaroorat, na alag download.

App mein aap ko milega: tamam categories (Bitter Truth, Sad Truth, Funny Truth, Crazy Truth, Fun Facts), quotes collections, aur YouTube Shorts tak seedha rasta.

Agar button kaam na kare: Chrome menu (⋮) → "Install app" / "Add to Home screen" chunein. iPhone par: Share → "Add to Home Screen".

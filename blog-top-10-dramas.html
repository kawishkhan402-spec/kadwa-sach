10 Hindi K-Drama Love Stories on Mafia Hearts KDrama

Agar aap ko Korean romance dramas pasand hain magar aap unhein Hindi/Urdu narration mein sunna chahte hain, to Mafia Hearts KDrama par 10 mukhtalif series chal rahi hain — har ek 45 episodes, har episode 6–9 minute, aur har kahani ka apna rang. Yeh raha mukammal guide:

1. Mafia Hearts

Channel ki signature series. Ek ruthless mafia boss aur ek masoom larki ki mohabbat — taqat, khauf aur dil ka takrao.

2. The CEO's Secret Bride

Contract marriage aur chhupa hua rishta. Karobar ki duniya mein dil ki jeet hogi ya haar?

3. My Dangerous Bodyguard

Hifazat ke liye aaya tha, mohabbat mein giraftar ho gaya. Action aur romance ka behtareen sangam.

4. The Surgeon's Hidden Past

Ek mashhoor doctor ka woh raaz jo saamne aaya to sab ki zindagi badal gayi. Mystery pasand karne walon ke liye.

5. Ex-Husband Regret

Pachtahwa aur doosra mauqa — kya guzra waqt wapas aa sakta hai?

6. Idol Secret Love

Ek superstar ki chhupi hui mohabbat — jahan ek ghalti se poora career khatre mein par sakta hai.

7. Reborn to Love

Doosri zindagi, doosra mauqa. Eun-ha is baar apni qismat khud likhegi.

8. Boss Secret Husband

Office ka sakht boss hi ghar ka shohar hai? Halki phulki romance aur mazahiya lamhat ke saath.

9. His Last Promise

Ek waada, ek intezar. Sab se zyada emotional series — tissue paas rakhein.

10. Replacement Bride

Kisi aur ki jagah bani dulhan — naye ghar mein mohabbat milegi ya imtihan?

Kahan se shuru karein?

Sab series ki playlists YouTube par maujood hain. Roz 2 naye episodes aate hain, is liye subscribe karna na bhoolein — aur roz ke aura Shorts bhi miss na karein.
